# Demo 10 - "A studio you can film"

Week 10 clip. Target length 40-50 s, 16:9, captions burned in. Shows the new studio layout: settings in a sheet, by hand; feature switches; and Show my hands, which this clip is filmed with from shot 4 on.

## Setup (before recording)

1. `pnpm dev`; open http://127.0.0.1:5173 in Edge, light theme (Windows set to light), sample project open.
2. Front light on both hands; Windows Studio Effects off. Start the camera from the camera view at the bottom right and check both hands are found close to 100% (Settings > Camera and tracking).
3. Settings > Features: everything on. Show my hands: off.
4. Recorder at 1080p, 30 fps, capturing the browser window.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Do | Voice-over / caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-5 s | The full-width model; the camera floats at the bottom right. | "The model gets the whole screen now." |
| 2 | 5-12 s | Point at the gear with the left hand (it lights amber), tap: the settings slide in. Open Features. | "Settings slide in, and my hands reach them." |
| 3 | 12-20 s | Tap **Create tools** off: long pinch with the left hand, no ring. Tap it on again. | "Every feature has a switch, for each release." |
| 4 | 20-26 s | Back, tap **Show my hands** on, close with the close button. White hands appear over the screen. | "And for you watching: my hands, drawn over the screen." |
| 5 | 26-40 s | Left hand: create ring, Wall; draw a wall while the right hand orbits. | "Now you can see what each hand does." |
| 6 | 40-45 s | Hide the camera view into the Camera pill; lower your hands. | "Next: the same gestures in Rhino 8." |

## Expected result

- The white hands sit on the pointers and never block a press.
- Create tools off removes them from the rings and keys; on brings them back.
- The camera keeps tracking while the settings open and close and while the view is hidden.

## Check after recording

- The white hands read clearly against the light model and panels on a phone.
- No other windows, notifications or account details in frame.
