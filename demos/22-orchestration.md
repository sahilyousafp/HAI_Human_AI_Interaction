# Demo 22 - "My AI stopped modelling and started directing"

> A talk to camera over two pages: the decision in `docs/decisions.md` ("HAI orchestrates host applications") and the research table in `docs/research/host-connectors.md`. Nothing is built in this change; the clips of the next changes show it working.

Target length about 50 s, 16:9, captions burned in.

## Setup (before recording)

1. Open `docs/research/host-connectors.md` rendered (GitHub or the editor's preview), scrolled to "Short answer".
2. Have HAI open beside it with a Rhino model mirrored, Rhino's window minimised.
3. Recorder at 1080p, 30 fps.
