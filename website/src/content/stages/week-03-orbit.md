With a mouse and keyboard we were limited to one strict form of input. Free of the keyboard, our hands guide the UI, and that is where an autonomous modelling environment can flourish.

Hand tracking has a problem a mouse doesn't: your arms run out of room. The clutch and the flick solve it, and resting your elbows on the desk keeps your shoulders fresh.

Thanks to Mehrnaz Arshad for helping create the models used to test and validate the system.
